# LMS plugins

You can use Ink Player videos in your course lessons. First add the video to [Media](/media), then copy its shortcode from the media row — for example `[ink_player id="123"]`.

## LearnDash

1. Open a **Lesson** or **Topic**.
2. Add the **Ink Player** block to the content and pick your video. In the classic editor, paste the shortcode instead.
3. Click **Update**.

::: warning
Don't paste the shortcode into **Video Progression → Video URL** — it won't show there. Keep the player in the lesson content.
:::

## LifterLMS

1. Open a **Lesson**.
2. Add the **Ink Player** block to the content and pick your video. In the classic editor, paste the shortcode instead.
3. Click **Update**.

::: warning
Don't use the lesson's **Video Embed URL** field — it only accepts links from sites like YouTube and Vimeo. Keep the player in the lesson content.
:::

## Tutor LMS

1. Open your course in the **Course Builder** and edit a lesson.
2. Under **Video**, choose **Shortcode** as the source.
3. Paste your shortcode, for example `[ink_player id="123"]`.
4. Save the lesson.
